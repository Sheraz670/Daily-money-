Calculate Your Tip

Enter your bill amount, choose a tip percentage and specify how many people will share the bill. The calculator shows the tip, total bill and estimated amount per person.

How to Use the Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the restaurant bill before adding the tip.
  2. Select a suggested tip rate or enter your own percentage.
  3. Enter the number of people sharing the bill.
  4. Choose a currency symbol and select Calculate Tip.
  5. Review the tip amount, total and per-person estimate.

The calculator assumes the tip is calculated on the full bill amount you enter. If your restaurant calculates tips before tax, enter the appropriate pre-tax amount.

Tip Calculator Formula

Tip amount = Bill amount × (Tip percentage ÷ 100)

Total bill = Bill amount + Tip amount

Cost per person = Total bill ÷ Number of people

Example: A $50 restaurant bill

If the bill is $50 and you choose a 20% tip:

  • Tip: $50 × 0.20 = $10
  • Total: $50 + $10 = $60
  • If three people share it equally: $60 ÷ 3 = $20 per person

This is an estimate. Local tipping customs and restaurant policies differ, and some bills already include a service charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

Tip expectations vary by country, service type and local custom. In some places, 15–20% is common for table service, while other countries have different practices. Check local guidance when travelling.

Can I split the bill between several people?

Yes. Enter the number of people, and the calculator divides the bill plus tip equally. It does not account for people ordering different amounts.

Can I calculate a custom tip percentage?

Yes. Choose the custom percentage option and enter the rate you want to use.

Does this calculator include sales tax?

It calculates the tip on the bill amount you enter. Whether that amount includes sales tax depends on what you enter.

What if the bill already includes a service charge?

Check your receipt before adding another tip. The appropriate amount depends on the restaurant's policy and local customs.
