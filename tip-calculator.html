How Does a Tip Calculator Work?

A tip calculator estimates the tip based on your bill and selected percentage. It adds the tip to the original bill and divides the total by the number of people.

Tip Calculation Formula

Tip = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip

Each Person Pays = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Example

If your bill is $50 and you choose a 15% tip, your tip is $7.50 and the total bill is $57.50. One person would pay $57.50.
