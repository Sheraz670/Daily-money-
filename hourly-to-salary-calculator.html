How to Calculate Salary to Hourly Pay

The estimated hourly rate is calculated by dividing your annual salary by the total number of hours you work in a year.

Formula: Hourly rate = Annual salary ÷ (Hours per week × Working weeks per year)

For example, an annual salary of $52,000 with 40 working hours per week for 52 weeks equals an estimated hourly rate of $25 before taxes and deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert annual salary to hourly pay?

Divide your annual salary by your scheduled weekly hours multiplied by the number of working weeks in a year.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator estimates gross pay before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, and other deductions.

What if I do not work 40 hours per week?

Enter your actual average weekly working hours to calculate an estimate based on your schedule.

Is the result exact take-home pay?

No. This is an estimate. Actual earnings can vary with unpaid leave, bonuses, overtime, and other compensation.
