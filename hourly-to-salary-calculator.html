Convert Hourly Pay to Salary

Enter your hourly wage, weekly working hours and working weeks per year to estimate gross earnings before taxes and deductions.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your hourly wage before deductions.
  2. Enter your usual working hours per week.
  3. Enter the number of weeks you expect to work per year.
  4. Select a currency and press Calculate Salary.

Use 52 weeks for a full year of paid work. Reduce the number if you expect unpaid leave or seasonal work.

Hourly to Salary Formula

Weekly pay = Hourly wage × Hours per week

Annual pay = Hourly wage × Hours per week × Working weeks

Monthly average = Annual pay ÷ 12

Biweekly average = Annual pay ÷ 26

Example: $25 per hour

At $25 per hour, 40 hours per week and 52 working weeks:

  • Weekly gross pay: $1,000
  • Annual gross pay: $52,000
  • Average monthly gross pay: $4,333.33
  • Average biweekly gross pay: $2,000

These are estimates before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions and other deductions.

What Can Change Your Earnings?

Unpaid leave

Unpaid time off can reduce annual earnings. Adjust working weeks to reflect your expected paid working year.

Part-time work

You can enter fewer weekly hours. For example, $18 per hour for 20 hours each week over 50 weeks gives $18,000 in annual gross pay.

Overtime and bonuses

This calculator uses one hourly rate and does not automatically include overtime, bonuses, commissions or tips. Use the Overtime Calculator for a separate overtime estimate.

Gross pay versus take-home pay

Gross pay is earnings before deductions. Take-home pay depends on taxes, benefits, contributions and other payroll rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is $20 an hour per year?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $20 per hour equals $41,600 in annual gross pay before deductions.

How do I calculate monthly salary?

Multiply hourly pay by weekly hours and working weeks, then divide the annual result by 12.

Does this calculate take-home pay?

No. This tool estimates gross pay before taxes and other deductions.

Can part-time workers use it?

Yes. Enter your usual weekly hours and working weeks to estimate your earnings.

Are overtime earnings included?

No. This calculator applies the same hourly rate to all entered hours.

About This Calculator

This calculator performs a basic wage conversion in your browser. It does not connect to an employer's payroll system or determine legal wage entitlement.
