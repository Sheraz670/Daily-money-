How It Works

This calculator estimates income from hourly pay, weekly working hours and working weeks per year. It does not automatically deduct taxes, insurance, unpaid leave or other deductions.

Salary Formulas

Weekly Income = Hourly Wage × Hours per Week

Annual Income = Weekly Income × Working Weeks per Year

Monthly Average = Annual Income ÷ 12

Example

At $20 per hour for 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year, estimated annual gross income is $41,600 before deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculate take-home pay?

No. The result is estimated gross income before taxes and other deductions.

Can I account for unpaid weeks?

Yes. Change the working weeks per year to reflect unpaid leave or the number of weeks you expect to work.
