Convert Hourly Pay to Salary

Enter your hourly wage and typical work schedule to estimate gross earnings over different periods. You can adjust weekly hours and the number of working weeks to match your schedule.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your hourly wage before taxes or deductions.
  2. Enter the number of hours you usually work each week.
  3. Enter how many weeks you expect to work during the year.
  4. Select a currency symbol and calculate your estimated pay.

Use 52 weeks for a full year without unpaid time off. Reduce the number of weeks if you expect unpaid leave or seasonal work. This estimate does not automatically include overtime, bonuses, commissions or paid leave adjustments.

Hourly to Salary Formula

Weekly gross pay = Hourly wage × Hours per week

Annual gross pay = Hourly wage × Hours per week × Working weeks per year

Average monthly gross pay = Annual gross pay ÷ 12

Example: $25 per hour

If you earn $25 per hour, work 40 hours per week and work 52 weeks per year:

  • Weekly gross pay: $25 × 40 = $1,000
  • Annual gross pay: $1,000 × 52 = $52,000
  • Average monthly gross pay: $52,000 ÷ 12 = $4,333.33

These figures are gross earnings before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions and other deductions. Monthly pay is an annual average; an actual paycheck may follow a different schedule.

What Can Change Your Annual Earnings?

Unpaid time off

If you do not get paid during holidays or leave, your annual earnings may be lower than an estimate based on 52 paid working weeks. Adjust the working-weeks field to estimate a shorter work year.

Part-time schedules

Part-time workers can use their usual weekly hours. For example, working 20 hours a week at $18 per hour for 50 weeks gives estimated annual gross earnings of $18,000.

Overtime and additional pay

This calculator assumes a single hourly rate. Overtime premiums, bonuses, tips and commissions are not included. Use the Overtime Calculator to estimate regular and overtime pay separately.

Gross pay versus take-home pay

Gross pay is earnings before deductions. Take-home pay is what remains after applicable taxes and other deductions. The actual amount depends on your location, tax situation, benefits and employer payroll rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is $20 an hour per year?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $20 per hour equals $41,600 in annual gross pay before deductions, unpaid leave or other adjustments.

How do I calculate monthly salary from hourly pay?

Multiply the hourly wage by weekly hours and working weeks per year, then divide the annual amount by 12. This gives average monthly gross earnings.

Does this calculator calculate take-home pay?

No. It estimates gross pay. Tax and payroll deductions vary, so the result should not be treated as an exact paycheck amount.

Can I use this for part-time work?

Yes. Enter your actual weekly hours and expected working weeks. The estimate will adjust to those values.

Are overtime earnings included?

No. The calculation uses one hourly rate for all entered hours. Overtime rules vary by jurisdiction and employment agreement.
