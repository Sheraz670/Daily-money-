Calculate Your Discount

Shopping discounts can be confusing when prices and percentages vary. Use this calculator to estimate the amount saved and the price after a discount.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the item's original price before the discount.
  2. Enter the discount percentage shown by the retailer.
  3. Choose the currency symbol for your display.
  4. Select Calculate Savings to see the discount amount and final price.

This calculator applies one percentage discount to the original price. It does not automatically include sales tax, shipping, coupons or additional discounts.

Discount Formula

Discount amount = Original price × (Discount percentage ÷ 100)

Sale price = Original price − Discount amount

Example: 25% off a $120 item

  • Original price: $120
  • Discount: $120 × 0.25 = $30
  • Price after discount: $120 − $30 = $90
  • You save $30 before any applicable taxes or extra charges.

The percentage is applied to the original price, not to a price that has already been reduced.

Understanding Shopping Discounts

What does 10% off mean?

A 10% discount reduces the original price by one tenth. For example, a $40 product receives a $4 discount and costs $36 before taxes or other charges.

How do two successive discounts work?

Successive discounts are usually applied one after another. For example, 20% off followed by another 10% off a $100 item gives a first price of $80 and a second price of $72. The total reduction is $28, or 28% of the original price, rather than 30%.

Does the lowest sale price always mean the best deal?

Not necessarily. Compare the final price, product quantity, quality, shipping fees, return policy and any conditions attached to the promotion. A large advertised percentage does not guarantee that an item is good value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 0.20 to find the saving, then subtract that amount from the original price. For a $50 item, the saving is $10 and the discounted price is $40.

Can a discount be 100%?

Yes. A 100% discount reduces the calculated sale price to zero, before any separate fees or charges.

Does the result include sales tax?

No. This tool calculates the price after the discount only. Use the Sales Tax Calculator separately if you need a tax estimate.

Can I use this calculator for any currency?

You can calculate with any currency as long as you enter the price in that currency. The symbol is a display choice; the tool does not convert exchange rates.

Is the displayed saving guaranteed at checkout?

No. Retailer terms, exclusions, rounding, taxes and other charges may change the final checkout amount. Confirm the final price with the seller.
