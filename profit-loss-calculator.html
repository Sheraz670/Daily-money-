What Is Profit and Loss?

Profit occurs when the selling price is higher than the total cost. A loss occurs when the selling price is lower than the total cost.

Formulas Used

  • Total cost: Cost price + additional costs.
  • Profit or loss: Selling price − total cost.
  • Profit or loss percentage: (Profit or loss ÷ total cost) × 100.
  • Profit margin: (Profit ÷ selling price) × 100.

Example

If an item costs $80 and you sell it for $100 with no additional costs, your profit is $20. Your profit percentage based on cost is 25%, while your profit margin based on sales is 20%.

Important Information

This calculator provides a simple estimate. For a business, actual profit may also depend on taxes, returns, fees, inventory costs, and other operating expenses.
