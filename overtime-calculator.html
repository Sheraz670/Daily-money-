How Overtime Pay Is Calculated

Overtime pay depends on your hourly wage, overtime hours and the multiplier used by your employer or required by applicable law.

Formula

Regular Pay = Hourly Wage × Regular Hours

Overtime Rate = Hourly Wage × Overtime Multiplier

Overtime Pay = Overtime Rate × Overtime Hours

Total Gross Pay = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay

Example

If your hourly wage is $20 and your overtime multiplier is 1.5, your estimated overtime rate is $30 per hour. Five overtime hours would equal $150 in overtime pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a 1.5 multiplier mean?

It means the overtime rate is one and a half times the regular hourly wage.

Is this calculator a legal pay determination?

No. Overtime rules vary by location, job and employment arrangement. Check the laws that apply to your situation and your employer's pay policy.
