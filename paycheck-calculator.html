How Does a Paycheck Calculator Work?

A paycheck calculator estimates how much money remains after taxes and other deductions are taken from your gross pay.

Enter the gross amount you receive each payday, select how often you are paid, and enter your estimated tax rates. The calculator subtracts estimated taxes and other deductions to show your take-home pay.

Important Information

Tax rates vary by country, state, income, and individual circumstances. This calculator uses the percentages you enter and does not determine your actual tax liability.
