What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines the time between a date of birth and another selected date. It can express the result in completed years, months, and days. This is useful for personal planning, school activities, forms, and checking how long it has been since a particular date.

The result depends on the dates entered. If you select a future date of birth or an end date before your birth date, the calculator will ask you to correct the dates.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to know your age.
  3. Tap “Calculate Age”.
  4. Read your completed age in years, months, and days, plus your next birthday information.

How Age Is Calculated

The calculator compares the birth date with the selected end date. It counts complete years first, then complete months after the last birthday, and finally the remaining days.

For example, someone born on 15 March 2000 is 26 years old on 15 March 2026. On 14 March 2026, they are still 25 years old.

Calendar months have different lengths, so a precise age in months and days is not always equivalent to a fixed number of days.

Understanding Your Result

  • Years: completed years since your birth date.
  • Months: additional complete calendar months after the last completed year.
  • Days: remaining days after counting complete years and months.
  • Days lived: total calendar-day difference between the two dates.
  • Next birthday: the next birthday date after the selected calculation date.

Leap years and different month lengths can affect the number of days between dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Enter your birth date and any later date, including a date in the past, to calculate how old you were then.

Does this calculator account for leap years?

Yes. It uses calendar dates, so February 29 and leap years are included in the date calculations.

Why do months have different day counts?

Calendar months range from 28 to 31 days. The years-months-days result follows calendar boundaries rather than treating every month as an equal number of days.

Can I use it to calculate someone else's age?

Yes. Enter that person's date of birth and the date on which you want to calculate their age. Only enter personal information when you have a legitimate reason to use it.

Is this result an official age record?

No. It is a date-based estimate. Official forms and eligibility rules may have their own requirements for documenting age.
