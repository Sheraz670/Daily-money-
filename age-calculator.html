How to Use the Age Calculator

Select your date of birth, then select the date on which you want to know your age. Press Calculate Age to see your age in years, months, and days, along with the total number of days between the two dates.

How Is Age Calculated?

Age is calculated by comparing the birth date with the selected date. The result accounts for the different lengths of calendar months and leap years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my age on a past date?
Yes. Select any date on or after your date of birth.

Does this calculator account for leap years?
Yes. It uses calendar dates to calculate the age and the number of days between dates.
