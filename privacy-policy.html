Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Money ("we," "our," or "the website") may handle information when you visit our website and use our online calculators.

By using the website, you acknowledge this policy. Please do not enter confidential or sensitive personal information into calculator fields.

These values are used to calculate the result requested by the visitor. Do not assume that every value is stored only on your device; this can depend on how the website and its services are implemented.

We do not claim to collect every type of information listed above. The information actually collected depends on the services in use.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services may store in a visitor's browser. They can be used for website functionality, preferences, analytics, security, or advertising.

Daily Money may use cookies or similar technologies if enabled by the hosting, analytics, or advertising services we use. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

Analytics and Advertising

If we enable an analytics service, that service may process information about how visitors interact with our website to help us understand usage and improve our tools. Please refer to the relevant provider's privacy policy for details.

If advertising services, including Google AdSense, are used on this website, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertising as permitted by applicable settings and laws.

Advertising and analytics services are not necessarily active just because they are mentioned in this policy. Their actual use depends on our website configuration and account status.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy.
