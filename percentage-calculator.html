Percentage Formulas

1. Find a percentage of a number

Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: 20% of 150 = 0.20 × 150 = 30.

2. Find what percentage one number is of another

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example: 30 is what percentage of 150? The answer is (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%.

3. Calculate percentage change

Percentage change = ((New value − Original value) ÷ |Original value|) × 100

Example: A price rises from 100 to 125. The change is (125 − 100) ÷ 100 × 100 = 25% increase.

If the new value is lower than the original value, the result is a decrease. Percentage change cannot be calculated when the original value is zero.

Where Are Percentages Used?

Percentages are used in shopping discounts, sales tax, interest rates, test scores, business reports and budgeting. They help express a proportion relative to 100, making it easier to compare values of different sizes.

Percentages and percentage points

A change from 10% to 15% is an increase of 5 percentage points. Relative to the original 10%, it is a 50% increase. These describe different comparisons, so it is important to use the right term.

Why does the original value matter?

A percentage increase or decrease is measured against the original value. An increase of $20 on a $100 item is 20%, but an increase of $20 on a $200 item is 10%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 15% of a number?

Multiply the number by 0.15. For example, 15% of 80 is 12.

How do I find the percentage between two numbers?

To find what percentage one number represents of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. Make sure the whole is not zero.

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, then multiply by 100. A positive result indicates an increase.

Can the percentage be more than 100%?

Yes. For example, 150% of 40 is 60. A proportion can exceed 100% when the part is larger than the comparison value.

Does this calculator round the answer?

Results are displayed to up to four decimal places when needed. Small differences may occur when a result is rounded for display.
