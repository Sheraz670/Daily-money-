How to Use This Date Calculator

To find the difference between two dates, enter a start date and an end date. The calculator shows the number of days, complete weeks, and remaining days.

You can also enter a starting date and a number of days to find a future or past date. Choose Add days or Subtract days to get your result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculator handle leap years?
Yes. It uses calendar dates, including leap days.

Can I calculate dates in the past?
Yes. Use the Subtract days option to calculate an earlier date.

Are the results official deadlines?
No. For legal, financial, or official deadlines, check the applicable rules about weekends and holidays.
