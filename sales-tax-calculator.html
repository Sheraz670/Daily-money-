How Sales Tax Is Calculated

Sales tax is calculated by multiplying the taxable price by the tax rate expressed as a decimal.

Sales Tax = Original Price × (Tax Rate ÷ 100)

Total Price = Original Price + Sales Tax

Example

If an item costs $100 and the sales tax rate is 8%, the tax is $8 and the final price is $108.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is sales tax included in the original price?

This calculator assumes the original price is before tax. It adds the calculated tax to show the estimated final price.

Can I use a different tax rate?

Yes. Enter any rate from 0% to 100%. Make sure you use the correct rate for your location and purchase.

Does every product have the same sales tax?

No. Some jurisdictions exempt certain goods or apply different rates. Local rules determine the applicable tax.

Is this an official tax calculation?

No. This tool provides a general estimate. It does not determine legal tax obligations or account for every local tax rule.
