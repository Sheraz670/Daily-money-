How to Calculate Sales Tax

Sales tax is a percentage added to the price of certain goods or services. Enter the applicable rate for your location and purchase.

Formula

Tax Amount = Price Before Tax × Tax Rate ÷ 100

Final Price = Price Before Tax + Tax Amount

Example

If an item costs $100 before tax and the sales tax rate is 8%, the tax is $8 and the final price is $108.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every purchase have sales tax?

No. Tax rules and exemptions vary by country, state, region, product and service.

Does this find my local tax rate?

No. Enter the tax rate that applies to your purchase. This calculator does not automatically identify local tax rules.
