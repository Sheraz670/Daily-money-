What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measurement calculated from a person's weight and height. It can help provide a general indication of weight category, but it does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

BMI Categories for Adults

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9: Healthy weight range
  • 25 to 29.9: Overweight
  • 30 or above: Obesity range

How Is BMI Calculated?

For metric measurements, BMI equals weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared.

For US measurements, BMI equals weight in pounds divided by height in inches squared, multiplied by 703.

Important Limitations

These categories are intended for adults. BMI is not a diagnosis and may not accurately represent health for children, teenagers, pregnant people, athletes, or all body types. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for individual advice.
