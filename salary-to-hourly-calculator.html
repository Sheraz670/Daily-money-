How It Works

This calculator divides annual gross salary by the total number of working hours in a year. The result is an estimate before taxes and other deductions.

Formula

Total Annual Hours = Hours per Week × Working Weeks per Year

Hourly Wage = Annual Salary ÷ Total Annual Hours

Example

An annual salary of $52,000, working 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, equals an estimated gross hourly wage of $25.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this my take-home hourly pay?

No. The result is gross hourly pay before taxes, insurance and other deductions.

Can my hourly rate change?

Yes. The estimate changes when your annual salary, weekly hours or working weeks change.
