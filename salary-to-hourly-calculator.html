Convert Annual Salary to Hourly Pay

Use this calculator to estimate how much an annual salary equals per hour, week, two-week pay period and month. The calculation uses the working hours and paid weeks you enter.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual gross salary before deductions.
  2. Enter the number of hours you normally work each week.
  3. Enter the number of weeks covered by the annual salary.
  4. Choose your preferred currency symbol and calculate.

Use 52 weeks for a typical full-year salary. If you work a seasonal schedule, use an appropriate number of paid weeks. The result assumes your salary relates to the work schedule entered.

Salary to Hourly Formula

Hourly equivalent = Annual salary ÷ (Weekly hours × Paid weeks per year)

Weekly equivalent = Annual salary ÷ Paid weeks per year

Average monthly equivalent = Annual salary ÷ 12

Example: $52,000 annual salary

Suppose your annual gross salary is $52,000, and you work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks.

  • Annual hours: 40 × 52 = 2,080 hours
  • Hourly equivalent: $52,000 ÷ 2,080 = $25 per hour
  • Weekly average: $52,000 ÷ 52 = $1,000
  • Monthly average: $52,000 ÷ 12 = $4,333.33

These are gross-pay estimates before taxes, benefits, retirement contributions or other deductions.

Why Working Hours Matter

Full-time and part-time schedules

The same annual salary can represent different hourly equivalents depending on working hours. For example, a $52,000 salary spread across 2,080 hours equals $25 per hour, while the same salary across 1,560 hours equals about $33.33 per hour.

Paid leave and unpaid leave

If your annual salary remains unchanged while you take paid leave, your contractual hourly equivalent may differ from a simple calculation based on hours physically worked. This calculator uses the schedule you enter and does not interpret your employment contract.

Gross salary versus take-home pay

Gross salary is the amount before deductions. Your actual take-home pay depends on tax rules, benefits, insurance, pension contributions and other payroll arrangements. This calculator does not estimate those deductions.

Overtime and bonuses

Annual salary may not include additional bonuses or overtime. If you receive variable compensation, consider whether to include it in the annual figure before comparing jobs. Overtime rules depend on your location and employment terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $50,000 a year is approximately $24.04 per hour before deductions.

How do I convert a monthly salary to an hourly rate?

Multiply the monthly salary by 12 to estimate annual salary, then divide by your annual working hours. For a standard 40-hour week over 52 weeks, annual working hours are 2,080.

Does this result show my take-home hourly pay?

No. It calculates a gross hourly equivalent. Taxes and other deductions can reduce the amount you receive.

Why is my hourly rate different from my employer's figure?

Your employer may use contractual hours, paid leave rules, payroll periods or compensation components that differ from the assumptions entered here.

Can I compare two job offers with this calculator?

Yes, as a starting point. Compare hours, paid leave, benefits, bonuses, commuting expenses and other employment conditions before making a decision.
