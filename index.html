Understanding Common Money Calculations

How is annual pay estimated from hourly wages?

A simple estimate multiplies the hourly rate by hours worked per week and the number of working weeks in a year. For example, $20 per hour for 40 hours per week over 52 weeks gives $41,600 in annual gross pay before taxes, unpaid leave or other adjustments.

How is a discount calculated?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage expressed as a decimal to find the amount saved. Subtract that saving from the original price to get the discounted price. A 20% discount on a $50 item saves $10, leaving a price of $40 before any applicable tax.

How do you calculate profit margin?

Subtract total costs from revenue to find profit. Divide profit by revenue and multiply by 100 to calculate profit margin. If revenue is zero, a percentage margin cannot be calculated using this formula.

What affects a loan payment?

For a standard fixed-rate loan, the payment depends on the amount borrowed, periodic interest rate and number of payments. Fees, variable rates, insurance and lender-specific rules may change the actual amount payable.
