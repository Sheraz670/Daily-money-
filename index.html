Formula

For a fixed-rate loan, payment = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly payments.

Worked example

A $10,000 loan at 6% APR over 36 months has an estimated fixed monthly payment of about $304.22, before fees.
