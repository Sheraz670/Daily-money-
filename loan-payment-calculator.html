How Is a Loan Payment Calculated?

For a standard fixed-rate loan with monthly payments, the monthly payment depends on the amount borrowed, the monthly interest rate, and the number of payments.

When the interest rate is greater than zero, the calculator uses the standard amortizing-loan payment formula. When the interest rate is zero, it divides the loan amount equally across all monthly payments.

Example

For a $20,000 loan over five years at a fixed annual interest rate of 6.5%, the calculator estimates the monthly payment and the total interest paid over 60 payments.

What Is Not Included?

This estimate does not include lender fees, insurance, taxes, variable interest rates, or extra payments. Your lender's actual repayment schedule may differ.

Frequently Asked Question

Does a longer loan term reduce the monthly payment?
Usually, yes. However, a longer term can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.
