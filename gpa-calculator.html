How Is GPA Calculated?

Grade Point Average (GPA) is commonly calculated by multiplying each course's grade points by its credit hours, adding those results, and dividing by the total credit hours.

Formula: GPA = Total (Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours.

Standard 4.0 Grade Scale

  • A: 4.0 points
  • A−: 3.7 points
  • B+: 3.3 points
  • B: 3.0 points
  • B−: 2.7 points
  • C+: 2.3 points
  • C: 2.0 points
  • C−: 1.7 points
  • D+: 1.3 points
  • D: 1.0 point
  • F: 0 points

Important Information

Grading scales differ between schools, colleges, and countries. Some institutions treat plus/minus grades, repeated courses, withdrawals, and pass/fail classes differently. Check your institution's official policy for an official GPA.
