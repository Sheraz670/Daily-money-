How Is GPA Calculated?

A weighted grade point average accounts for the credit hours assigned to each course. Courses with more credit hours have a greater effect on the final GPA.

Formula:

GPA = Total of (Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours

Example

Suppose you earn 4.0 grade points in a 3-credit course and 3.0 grade points in a 2-credit course.

Weighted GPA = ((4.0 × 3) + (3.0 × 2)) ÷ (3 + 2) = 3.60

Frequently Asked Questions

What does GPA mean?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It summarizes academic performance as a numerical average based on the grading system used by a school or university.

Is a 4.0 GPA always the maximum?

No. Some institutions use a 4.0 scale, while others use different scales or weighted systems. This calculator is designed for a standard 0.0–4.0 scale.

Does this calculate cumulative GPA?

It calculates a weighted GPA from the courses entered. To estimate a cumulative GPA, include all relevant courses and their credit hours, provided they use a compatible grading scale.

Why might my school's GPA be different?

Your institution may use different grade-point values, rounding rules, repeated-course policies, or special weighting. This result is an estimate, not an official academic record.
